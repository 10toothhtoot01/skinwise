# Own-kit planning

The planning context concerns choosing among products already owned for an occasion. Digital appearance and physical results remain different things.

## Language

**Owned kit**:
The products a person records as already available and intends to consider.
_Avoid_: Shopping basket, recommended products

**Feasible combination**:
A nonempty combination of supported products that fits the person's entered steps, limits and summed time estimates. It is not a verified physical outcome.
_Avoid_: Best look, suitable look

**Decision context**:
The occasion, owned kit and constraints under which a person expresses a preference or chooses a plan.
_Avoid_: Render specification

**Prepared portrait**:
The same resized original used independently for each visual rehearsal.
_Avoid_: Generated source, previous preview

**Render specification**:
The precise digital makeup settings applied to a prepared portrait. Renaming a product or changing a time limit does not change these settings.
_Avoid_: Product match, decision context

**Chosen plan**:
An explicit choice from entered constraints, an available preview, or the no-added-makeup opt-out, with its basis preserved.
_Avoid_: Inferred winner, beauty recommendation

**Recovery receipt**:
Private access to a previously started preview task until that access expires. It is not a new render or a permanent saved image.
_Avoid_: Shareable plan
